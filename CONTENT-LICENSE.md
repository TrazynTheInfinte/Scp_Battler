## Rules for adding content

- Lore and descriptions are paraphrased, never pasted wholesale, and every SCP
  used is credited (article, author(s), link) in the in-game Credits screen.
- An image from a wiki article may be used **only** if that article's licensing
  box lists the image as CC BY-SA 3.0 (or compatible / public domain). Each one
  is credited individually. Images listed as "used with permission" or with no
  license (e.g. the original SCP-173 sculpture photo) must not be used.
- Otherwise use original art or placeholders.
